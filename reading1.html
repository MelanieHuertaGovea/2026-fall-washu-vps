Reading Response 1

Reading Info

I found the reading interesting because it shows how websites can be more than just a place to find information. The authors argue that older restaurant websites had more personality because they were often designed specifically for the restaurant instead of using standardized templates. I especially liked the idea that a website can communicate the atmosphere and identity of a physical place before you even visit it. At the same time, the reading recognizes that creative websites require time and labor, which can be difficult for restaurants to afford. I think the main challenge is finding a balance between making websites easy to update and giving designers enough freedom to make them unique.
