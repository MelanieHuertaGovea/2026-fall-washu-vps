Reading Response 3

What stood out to me most about the reading was the idea that a website does not have to be perfect or serve one specific purpose. I liked Schwulst’s comparison of a website to a garden because a garden takes time to grow and can constantly change depending on what you add to it. I think this is similar to how a person’s identity and interests change over time. Instead of thinking of a website as something that needs to be finished, I like the idea of it being a space that can grow with you. I also found her criticism of social media interesting because most people use platforms like Instagram to represent themselves online, but those platforms are controlled by companies. Creating your own website gives you more freedom to decide how you want to present yourself and what you want your online space to look like.
